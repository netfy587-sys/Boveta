See What’s Inside: The Animal World

Animals live in every corner of our planet, from frozen poles to warm oceans. Each one has special ways of finding food, staying safe and raising young. Learning about them helps us understand nature and our place in it.

Every animal is perfectly suited to the place it calls home.

Habitats shape bodies

Thick fur keeps polar animals warm, long legs help desert animals stay cool, and webbed feet make swimming easy. Body shape often tells you where an animal lives.

Food chains connect life

Plants make food from sunlight, small animals eat plants, and larger animals eat the small ones. Every link matters, so a change in one place can affect many others.

Animals communicate

Birds sing, bees dance and whales call across the ocean. Studying these signals shows how animals share warnings, find friends and care for their young.

We can all be good neighbors

Keeping water clean, planting flowers for bees and watching wildlife quietly are small ways to help. Explore New Possibilities by spending ten minutes outdoors and noticing one animal.
